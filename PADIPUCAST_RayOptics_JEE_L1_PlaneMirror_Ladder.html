Plane mirror

All 12 plane-mirror questions from the ALLEN JEE module, put in the same order as your notes: law of reflection → curved surface → where the image can be seen → two inclined mirrors → retracing (nθ = 90°) → parallel mirrors → triangle of mirrors → velocity of image.

Each source question is a ladder: 3 build-up rungs, then the original question (★) with its exercise number. Every ladder is marked DISCUSS IN CLASS or HOMEWORK. 12 ladders × 4 rungs = 48 questions (7 class, 5 homework).

One idea runs through most of these: work with the glancing angle (angle with the mirror). It is the same going in and coming out, and in two-mirror problems the glancing angles and the mirror angle form a triangle.
