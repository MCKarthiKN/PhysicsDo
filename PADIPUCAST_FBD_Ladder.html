Free Body Diagrams
Who is touching this body?

Slot 1 of the Newton's Laws flow. Every free-body-diagram question from the chapter, put in one climbing order. The sequence goes: which forces are really there → action and reaction → the normal force and its direction → spheres, walls and corners → three spheres in a groove → what a string does → a pulley bolted to a block → a rod resting on a corner.

Each source question is a ladder: 3 build-up rungs, then the original question (★). Every ladder is marked DISCUSS IN CLASS or HOMEWORK. 8 ladders × 4 rungs = 32 questions. Take g = 10 m/s2 throughout.

One routine runs through all 8: walk around the body and ask who is touching it. Each toucher gives one force, and the kind of contact fixes its direction. Then add gravity, and stop. A force the body applies to something else never goes in its own diagram.
