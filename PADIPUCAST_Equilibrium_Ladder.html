Point Equilibrium
Resolve, then balance

Slot 2 of the Newton's Laws flow. Every point-equilibrium question from the chapter, put in one climbing order. The sequence goes: what equilibrium means → the triangle of forces → finding a missing force → components with an unknown → a stack of blocks → hanging by a string → why a sagging string is safer → the 3-4-5 pull → pushing a rope at its middle → a string round a log → three blocks over two pulleys.

Each source question is a ladder: 3 build-up rungs, then the original question (★). Every ladder is marked DISCUSS IN CLASS or HOMEWORK. 11 ladders × 4 rungs = 44 questions. Take g = 10 m/s2 throughout.

One routine runs through all 11: draw the forces at the point, pick two directions (usually vertical and horizontal), and write ΣF = 0 along each. Do the vertical one first — it is nearly always the one that has only the weight and one unknown in it.
