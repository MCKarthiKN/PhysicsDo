Energy, Intensity and Radiation Pressure

Electromagnetic Waves, Lecture 4 — how much a wave actually carries. Energy density and the equal split between E and B, the master formula I = ½ε₀cE₀² and its four rearrangements, the inverse-square law, and radiation pressure. Every source question is a real JEE Main PYQ from the chapter bank, turned into a ladder: 3 build-up rungs, then the original exam question (★). Each ladder is marked DISCUSS IN CLASS or HOMEWORK. 20 ladders × 4 rungs = 80 questions.

Years covered: 2014, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2026. Two constants are worth memorising before you start: ε₀c = 2.655 × 10−3 and ½ε₀c = 1.33 × 10−3 — they turn most of these into a single multiplication. Ladders 4 and 5 are the same question asking for the electric part and then the total, so the factor changes from ¼ to ½; teach them together.
