From the first hit to the oblique hit

Your collision notes, put in one climbing order — the same order you teach it. The flow goes: what a collision is → kinds of collision → deformation and restitution → momentum is conserved → e, the coefficient of restitution → elastic collision and its special cases → inelastic and perfectly inelastic → bullet in a pendulum bob, particle on a wedge → ball bouncing on the floor → oblique collision.

Each idea is a ladder: 3 build-up rungs, then the ★ question from your notes (Illustration, JEE Main PYQ, or a concept check). Every ladder is marked DISCUSS IN CLASS or HOMEWORK. 17 ladders × 4 rungs = 68 questions. Take g = 10 m/s2 and right as positive.

One rule runs through all 17: momentum is conserved in every collision. Kinetic energy is conserved only when e = 1. e = speed of separation ÷ speed of approach — it tells you how much bounce is left.
