From one point to a disc with a hole

Every problem from your COM notes, put in one climbing order that follows the four calculation types in the notes: what COM is → discrete masses (line, plane, triangle, 3D vectors) → continuous rods with dm = λdx (uniform, the three H.W rods, JEE 2020) → the half ring and half disc → compound bodies → truncated bodies → motion of the COM.

Each source question is a ladder: 3 build-up rungs, then the question from the notes (★). Every ladder is marked DISCUSS IN CLASS (worked in the notes) or HOMEWORK (set or left unsolved in the notes). 21 ladders × 4 rungs = 84 questions.

One formula runs through all 21: xcom = Σmx / Σm. For a continuous body, Σ becomes ∫ and m becomes dm. For a compound body, each part becomes one point mass at its own COM. For a cut body, the removed piece goes in with a minus sign.
