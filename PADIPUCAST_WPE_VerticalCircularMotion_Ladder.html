Vertical Circular Motion
One equation, then energy

Every vertical-circle question from the Work, Energy & Power JEE Main PYQ bank (2021–2023), put in one climbing order. The sequence follows the board work: the radial equation → T(θ) and its three special cases → energy on the circle → the pendulum → looping the loop → the 6mg gap → the three speed regimes → losing contact → curved tracks → the horizontal circle → a rough circle.

Each source PYQ is a ladder: 3 build-up rungs, then the original question (★) with its year and shift. Every ladder is marked DISCUSS IN CLASS or HOMEWORK. 10 ladders × 4 rungs = 40 questions. Take g = 10 m/s2 throughout.

One method runs through all ten: write the radial equation at the point you care about (which way is the centre?) → use energy, v² = u² − 2gh, to link two points → if something is about to let go, set the contact force to zero. T = 0 for a string, N = 0 for a surface. Tension and normal force never do work; only gravity and friction do.
