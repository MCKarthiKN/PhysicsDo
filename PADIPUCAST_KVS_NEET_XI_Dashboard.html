KVS Class 11 NEET results

ALLEN centre 6251, Madurai. Three tests from the NEET Nurture (WD-WE Phase 1 & 2) series.

    About this data
    • Marks are copied from the ALLEN provisional result sheets (centre 6251): Minor Test 4 (12 Aug), Minor Test 5 (2 Sep) and Major Test 2 (23 Sep 2026). Each subject is out of 180, total out of 720.
    • TN & PY average and highest come from the summary box at the end of each sheet. They cover all Tamil Nadu and Puducherry students, not only this centre.
    • "AB" means the sheet marks the student absent. "Not in sheet" means the name is not printed at all. The Minor 5 sheet has no absent list, so a missing name there most likely means absent.
    • Minor 5: the centre-rank column was not readable in the scan, so centre ranks for that test are worked out from the totals.
    • Balan B, Minor 5: the subject marks sit in a blurred part of the scan. The total (503) is sure because it matches the % and state rank, but one subject mark may be off by a few marks.
